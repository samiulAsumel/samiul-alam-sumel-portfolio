Operational areas

Port operations, seen from the inside

Select an area. Each lists what the work actually involved, in plain international terms.

Port operations

Cargo operations

  • Cargo movement and cargo handling records.
  • Cargo dwell-time monitoring across terminal locations.
  • General cargo: non-containerized cargo, handled and billed alongside vehicles.
  • Operational documentation for cargo movements.

Container workflows

  • Container tallying.
  • Containerized cargo delivery workflows.
  • Container statistics reporting.
  • Stuffing, delivery and shipping bill workflows.

Vehicle operations

  • Vehicle delivery workflows and vehicle-related operational records.
  • Vehicle and cargo position tracking across warehouse, shed and yard.
  • Car import and delivery statistics, and vehicle balance records.
  • Customs-related vehicle reporting, including auction vehicles.

Billing and commercial operations

  • Wharf-rent (port facility usage charge) billing: slab-wise charge calculation, VAT and levy, and the inside / outside cargo split.
  • Vehicle, general cargo, and stuffing, delivery and shipping-related billing; demurrage.
  • Advance billing estimates and billing records.
  • Applies the port’s published tariff; does not set tariff policy.

Revenue and operational reporting

  • Daily and monthly revenue reports and cumulative income statements.
  • Monthly Traffic Department reporting for port authority management.
  • Container, cargo, vehicle and jetty statistics.
  • Excel-based data preparation and record checking.

Documentation and administrative workflows

  • Operational and billing documentation, and reports.
  • Bank-deposit (challan) workflow and monthly statements.
  • Customs-related reporting.
  • Operational records.

Operational coordination

  • Customs & freight-forwarding (C&F) agents: documentation, billing queries and advance payment planning.
  • Shipping agents and stevedoring stakeholders.
  • Customs-related stakeholders and port authority management.
  • Coordination only; no authority over external organisations.

Data and reporting

  • Turning daily operational records into structured, recurring reports.
  • Cumulative and month-by-month figures kept consistent across billing, vehicle balances and statistics.
  • Record checking before figures are reported.

Process automation

  • Repetitive calculation and reporting steps identified while doing the work.
  • First structured in Excel, then rebuilt as small web tools.
  • Result: the Port Billing System, CarView and OT Bill Automation, plus OpsFlow as a product project.
