Engineering / AI Engineering

AI Is the Accelerator

Engineering is the responsibility.

AI is part of my engineering workflow. It is not the entire identity.

I'm a Product Engineer who uses AI-assisted development to build practical operational software — not an "AI expert" building revolutionary AI systems. My strongest evidence: real domain knowledge, real shipped software, real daily users. The honest tiering in the Skills section shows exactly where AI was involved in each project.

AI helps produce code faster. It does not decide what the correct product should do.

Accelerates implementation Gets me from a written requirement to working code faster, especially on backend and data work.
Helps explore solutions Useful for reasoning through implementation problems and prototyping before committing to an approach.
Requirements still come from the real problem No amount of AI assistance replaces sitting with the person who does the work and writing down what they actually need.
Business rules still need to be understood Slab charges, VAT, levy, cumulative OT — a model can't infer these; I have to know them and specify them.
The system still needs validation Every business rule and edge case gets manually tested against reality before anything ships.
